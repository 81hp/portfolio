About Me

My story

It all started when I created my first Minecraft server. Of course it did terribly, but it got me started with Skript. I did not stop, and after one more failed attempt one of my servers blew up: PrimalMines.

I've owned 5 Minecraft servers in total. Two of them did pretty well, and both were PrimalMines. Season 1 did best, around 2-3 years ago. Recently I made season 2, but I had been out of the server owner game for a while, so I based it on what box servers were like 2-3 years ago. It did pretty decently, but it had the potential to be much more. That was the final straw, and I've stopped owning servers.

What I liked about owning a server was seeing players have fun. That kept me motivated. What I liked most, though, was developing them. Creating cool systems was the most fun ever.

These days I mostly develop things here and there, but it's mostly calm.
